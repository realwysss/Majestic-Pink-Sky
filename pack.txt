# Majestic Pink Sky

A breathtaking, high-resolution (6K) custom sky overlay designed for maximum visual quality and performance. 

## Features:
- **Ultra High-Definition**: Crystal clear, 6144x4096 resolution ensures absolutely zero pixelation even when zoomed in.
- **Majestic Aesthetics**: Features a gorgeous pastel pink and blue gradient, accented by vibrant, fluffy pink cherry blossom clouds.
- **Clean and Static**: Intentionally non-rotating to provide a consistent, beautiful backdrop for your world.
- **Seamless Integration**: Fully compatible with OptiFine (Custom Skies required).

## Requirements:
- OptiFine (Ensure Custom Skies is turned ON in Video Settings -> Details)
- OR any compatible custom sky mod (like FabricSkyboxes/Nuit) that supports OptiFine properties.

## Recommended:
Pairs incredibly well with PvP texture packs, shaders with skybox blending disabled, or just for a relaxing, cute vibe in your survival world!
